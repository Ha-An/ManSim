# Local-Only Research Documents

Paper manuscripts, chapter drafts, appendices, manuscript figures, and ADP
formulation drafts must stay local. Keep new paper content under `docs/paper/`.
Never stage, commit, push, or force-add these documents, including their rendered
PDF/HTML versions. Preserve local copies when removing them from Git tracking.

Before any commit or push, inspect staged paths for research documents. The
experiment implementation and general software usage documentation may remain
versioned; they are distinct from the paper manuscripts.
